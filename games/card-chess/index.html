Tap each card to cycle: Black · White · Wait. Black keeps 2, White gets 2, 1 waits. White moves first.

How to play

  • Tap one of your two cards, then a piece — legal squares light yellow (for the Jumper, the piece being leapt lights red) — then tap the destination.
  • Your played card goes to the WAIT tile and the waiting card joins your hand.
  • Buttons: ↺ menu  ·  UNDO take back a move  ·  ? rules.
