Black and White 흑과 백

  • You each hold 9 tiles, 0–8. Even numbers are black (0,2,4,6,8), odd are white (1,3,5,7).
  • Over 9 rounds, you both secretly pick one tile and lock it in before the timer runs out. If you don't lock in time, a random remaining tile is played for you.
  • The higher number wins the round (+1 point); a tie scores nothing.
  • You only ever see your opponent's tile colour and whether you won — never their exact number, even afterwards. Track what they've spent to read what's left.
  • Most points after 9 rounds wins. Reaching 5 clinches it. Equal points is a draw.
