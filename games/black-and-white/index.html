Black and White 흑과 백

  • You each hold 9 tiles, 0–8. Even numbers are black (0,2,4,6,8), odd are white (1,3,5,7).
  • Over 9 rounds, you each play one tile and the higher number wins the round (+1 point); a tie scores nothing.
  • Round 1, you both lock in secretly at the same time. After that, the winner of the previous round must choose first — and the loser gets to see that tile's colour before responding. (A tie means the next round is simultaneous again.)
  • If you don't lock in before the timer, a random remaining tile is played for you.
  • You only ever see your opponent's tile colour, never their exact number — even afterwards. Track what they've spent to read what's left.
  • Most points after 9 rounds wins. Reaching 5 clinches it. Equal points is a draw.
